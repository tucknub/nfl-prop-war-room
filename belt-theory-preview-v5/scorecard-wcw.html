System thesis

The pipeline was better than its reputation.
The finish was worse.

WCW did not fail because it lacked an internal ladder. It failed because that ladder fed a world-title system that repeatedly reverted to established veterans, faction politics and administrative resets.

The 1991–94 foundation balanced long reigns and genuine elevation. The Hogan expansion maximized continuity by closing the top. The final reboot created opportunity through collapse: an eight-day median and 75% of world reigns under 30 days.

WCW’s deepest success was talent identification. Its defining failure was top-level conversion and protection.
