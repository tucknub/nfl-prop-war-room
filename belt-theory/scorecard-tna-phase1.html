System thesis

TNA’s world-title history is not one continuous belt.

TNA first booked a licensed NWA lineage, then established a distinct company-owned championship in May 2007.

Keeping those histories separate reveals the real change: the first era was highly concentrated, while the company-owned title distributed champion-days across a much larger field.

TNA relied on outside prestige—but it also created more lasting top-level equity than its reputation suggests.

Phase 1 conclusion

Broader than the stereotype.

The licensed launch was concentrated. The company-owned title became far more distributed, and career-first major champions narrowly produced the majority of portfolio champion-days. The complete system grade waits for X-Division, tag and Knockouts evidence.
