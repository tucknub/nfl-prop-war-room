Final verdict

Same overall score.
Different strengths.

AEW finishes with the smallest possible numerical edge, but the difference is too narrow to call a meaningful overall winner.

AEW is stronger where a newer promotion most needs credibility: its flagship world title, post-win protection for newly elevated men and the combined Tag–Trios structure. WWE is stronger where decades of infrastructure matter: secondary-title depth, women’s breakthrough retention and a mature company-wide women’s tag division.

AEW has the slightly better prestige-centered system. WWE has the stronger women’s-development system and deeper long-running secondary infrastructure.

Bottom line

No meaningful overall winner.

AEW built the stronger prestige-centered men’s system. WWE built the stronger women’s-development system and deeper secondary infrastructure. The matched period ends almost exactly even.
