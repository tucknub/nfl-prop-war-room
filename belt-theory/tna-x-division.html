System thesis

Not a myth.
Not a conveyor belt.

One in five recognized X-Division champions later became a TNA-controlled world champion after holding X first.

From the world-title side, eleven of 46 promotion-world champions—23.9%—came through the X title. Those wrestlers account for 2,243 world champion-days, or 26.3% of the complete classified portfolio.

The X-Division mattered most as a long-term credibility platform, not as a seven-match cash-in mechanism.

Phase 2 conclusion

The ladder was real.
The gimmick was smaller.

TNA's signature title produced credible long-term world champions across more than two decades. Option C gave the system a memorable shortcut, but the deeper historical value came from ordinary accumulation of identity, visibility and trust.
