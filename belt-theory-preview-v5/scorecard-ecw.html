System thesis

Less chaotic than its image.
More selective than its mythology.

The declared-world era featured a 63-day median first reign, meaningful internal mobility and almost no dependence on already established world champions.

The strongest feeder was not the Television Championship. It was the tag division: seven standard world champions held tag gold first, versus four Television paths and one FTW path.

ECW’s titles identified and elevated talent. The promotion struggled to preserve world-title momentum after the breakthrough.
