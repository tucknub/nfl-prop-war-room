## Findings

- Every fixed week produced 10–12 default cards, within the requested approximate 8–15 range.
- The complete technical match set ranged from 39 to 78 rows, confirming that de-duplication and section caps materially reduce noise.
- No player appears twice in a default report. Secondary qualifying categories are preserved in the card archive and duplicate-assignment file.
- Week 2 uses one previous qualifying game because only Week 1 can be available; later replay weeks require at least two prior same-team qualifying games.
- Week 18 produced 11 default cards and did not bypass any threshold.
- The maximum same-team concentration in a replay was 3 cards. No manual player or teammate removal was applied.
- Category section caps, not hand-edited exclusions, keep each replay reviewable in approximately five minutes.
