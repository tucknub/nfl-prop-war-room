Final verdict

WWE wins the system.
ECW nearly wins prestige.

WWE’s advantage was balance: the least concentrated world title, the strongest breakthrough retention, competitive secondary titles and the broadest usable architecture.

ECW’s world title was the most stable and its tag division narrowly led the field. WCW built the deepest internal résumés but allowed its world-title center to become extraordinarily concentrated and short-reign-heavy.

Project conclusion

WWE had the best system.
ECW had the best argument.

WCW’s failure was not talent discovery. ECW’s strength was not merely atmosphere. WWE’s win was not total dominance. The matched data shows three distinct systems: WWE converted and distributed best, ECW protected core prestige best for its scale, and WCW built the deepest ladder before undermining its finish.
