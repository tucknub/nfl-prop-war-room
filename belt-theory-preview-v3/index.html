Our methodology

A model you can challenge.

We do not disguise judgment as science. Dates, classifications, thresholds and weights stay visible so readers can separate recorded facts from analytical choices.

01
Equal datesPromotions are compared across the same calendar windows.
02
Matched rolesWorld is compared with world; team systems are normalized by active seats.
03
Transparent pathwaysWe track which company titles came before the first company world title.
04
DurabilityA breakthrough is durable when the champion repeats or the first reign reaches 180 days.
05
SensitivityMajor conclusions are retested when the model weights change.
