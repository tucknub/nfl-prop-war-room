# Supporting Evidence Experience Design Decisions

- Findings precede controls, complete tables, and methodology.
- Mobile cards are the default narrow-screen structure; complete tables remain optional.
- Home selection, ranking, categories, limits, and thresholds remain unchanged; only presentation wording changed.
- Team, player, and game summaries reuse shared count-weighted calculations.
- High-Value Opportunities was merged into Scoring-Area Usage because the slices duplicated the same research question.
- Unsupported score, longest-play, and one-play concentration fields are omitted.
