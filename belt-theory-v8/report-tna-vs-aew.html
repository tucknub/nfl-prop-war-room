The verdict

Two valid systems.
One narrow result.

TNA wins because its secondary ladder, women’s system and internally created world-title equity outweigh AEW’s superior flagship focus and cleaner governance.

Why TNA won

Creation and depth

  • 85.5% of matched men’s world champion-days came from wrestlers without a prior major-world championship.
  • The X-Division and one rotating secondary lane produced a clearer ladder than AEW’s four overlapping secondary belts.
  • The women’s system combined stronger lower-title participation, conversion and durability.
  • TNA produced more matched first-time men’s world champions.
  • Its simpler title-role map generated a 97.5 architecture score.
Where AEW was better

Focus and continuity

  • The AEW World Championship earned the strongest individual dimension score.
  • Fewer short reigns and greater concentration created a deliberate dynastic identity.
  • Sanctioned lineage continuity was cleaner.
  • The tag-plus-trios ecosystem created broader team participation.
  • Governance remained more stable across the matched period.
