# Locked Product Decisions

These decisions are not subject to revision during Phase A.

- PropWar is an NFL Role & Usage Research product.
- Home should ultimately be the weekly discovery surface.
- Teams, Players, Games, Reports, and Explorer provide supporting evidence.
- The public product contains no odds or betting recommendations.
- The public product uses no `PLAY`, `LEAN`, or `PASS` labels.
- The public product has no universal player score.
- The public product does not claim that a role change is sustainable or persistent.
- RB carry-share and RB opportunity-share detectors remain private shadow research.
- WR and TE automated role-change detection remain retired.
- Sleeper is postponed.
- Opponent adjustment is excluded.
- Portrait usability is mandatory.
- The current production commit is `8b759f18c34708300acf5e3ef84d0e4cbbbde597`.
- The active phase is Phase A — Targeted Correctness Audit.
- No production deployment is authorized during this phase.

Audit findings must be recorded as evidence, issues, limitations, or backlog items. They must not silently rewrite these decisions.
